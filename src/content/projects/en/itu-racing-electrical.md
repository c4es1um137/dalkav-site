## The problem

A Formula Student electric car carries a high-voltage accumulator that has to be safe, light and serviceable, and it has to pass scrutineering before the car may drive at all. The rules set hard limits on voltage, energy, isolation and fusing, so every design choice in the battery and tractive system is checked against them.

## What I did

From 2022 to 2024 I was part of the Electrical Systems team, working on inverter–motor calibration, electrical enclosures, wiring harness design and accumulator packaging. I helped prepare the car's electrical systems for EV scrutineering, the inspection where judges physically check the high-voltage design. In 2024–25 I captained the team of 84 people. Over three seasons I attended five Formula Student competitions, and I now volunteer at Formula Student Türkiye, on the other side of the table: at FST 2026 I was an electrical scrutineer.

## Results

In my season as captain the team finished 11th overall at Formula SAE Italy (FSATA) 2025 and ranked 78th in the 2025 Formula Student World Ranking, the highest of any team from Türkiye.

## What I learned

Working backwards from the rules is faster than designing first and checking later; it is the subject of [my note on accumulator design](/notes/fs-accumulator-rules-first). Having been through technical inspection both as a team member and as a volunteer, I know the electrical and the mechanical side of an FS car; accumulator and EV inspection are where I know the most.
