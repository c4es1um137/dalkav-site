## The problem

A Formula Student electric car carries a high-voltage accumulator that has to be safe, light and serviceable, and it has to pass scrutineering before the car may drive at all. The rules set hard limits on voltage, energy, isolation and fusing, so every design choice in the battery and tractive system is checked against them. [TODO: the car or season this work was for]

## What I did

From 2022 to 2024 I was part of the Electrical Systems team, working on the high-voltage accumulator and the tractive-system electrics. I helped prepare the car's electrical systems for EV scrutineering, the inspection where judges physically check the high-voltage design. In 2024–25 I captained the team.
